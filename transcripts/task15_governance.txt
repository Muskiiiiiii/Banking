==========================================================================================
APPLICATION LAYER — risk classification
==========================================================================================
Risk level: High

Under the Low/Medium/High scheme this system is High risk. It reads loan-application records that contain financial data (loan amounts, application status and fraud-review flags) and the answers it gives to support staff can shape how a member's loan or fraud dispute is handled, which is exactly the 'financial data' case the scheme lists as High. Members may also type PAN, Aadhaar or bank-account numbers into the chat. It is not a Medium-risk ticket summariser because a wrong answer about prepayment charges, KYC or dispute deadlines can cost a member money or legal protection. The controls that follow from High risk are: PII masking before any model or log sees the text, least-autonomy tool access, a groundedness refusal, a second independent review team, a per-request cost cap, structured JSONL audit logs, and human escalation via the escalation score; the agent informs staff and never approves, rejects or disburses a loan itself.
