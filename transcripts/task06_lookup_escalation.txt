escalation_score = 0.6 * fraud + 0.4 * staleness
  fraud     = 1 if flagged_for_fraud_review else 0
  staleness = days_since_created / 30 for open statuses (Submitted, Under Review, Approved); 0 for Rejected/Disbursed

Open applications: 30 of 50
Sorted days_since_created (open): [0, 0, 2, 4, 4, 5, 8, 8, 9, 10, 10, 11, 11, 11, 14, 17, 17, 18, 19, 20, 20, 22, 22, 25, 26, 27, 27, 28, 30, 30]
80th percentile = 25.2 days
threshold = 0.4 * 25.2 / 30 = 0.336

Escalated: 11/50 (22%) -> {'stale open application': 3, 'fraud flag': 8}
