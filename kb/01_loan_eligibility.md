# Loan eligibility criteria by loan type
Personal loan applicants must be salaried or self-employed Indian residents aged 21 to 60 with a net monthly income of at least Rs 25,000. Home loan applicants can be aged 21 to 65 at loan maturity and must contribute at least 20% of the property value as down payment. Auto loans require a minimum net monthly income of Rs 20,000 and fund up to 90% of the on-road price of a new vehicle. Education loans need an Indian co-applicant and confirmed admission to a recognised course, while business loans require at least 3 years of business vintage and 2 years of filed income-tax returns.
